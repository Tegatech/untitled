# JAGAVILLE Assets

This directory is the working home for JAGAVILLE visual assets.

## Structure

### `references/`
Original visual references, scans, sketches, screenshots and historic materials that inform the identity. These are source references, not final brand assets.

Suggested contents:
- Original mask sketches
- Historic JAGAVILLE ID/licence designs
- Visa/document inspiration
- Event and cultural references
- Historic iluvsluts.com screenshots and artwork

### `logo/working/`
In-progress logo and wordmark explorations. Nothing in this folder should be treated as approved or production-ready.

### `logo/final/`
Approved master identity assets only.

Expected final formats:
- SVG master
- Black-on-white variant
- White-on-black variant
- Symbol-only mask
- JAGAVILLE wordmark
- Combined lockups
- Formal Republic of Jagaville lockup where required

### `documents/`
JAGAVILLE document-system assets and templates, including IDs, licences, permits, visas, passes, tickets and related state/worldbuilding material.

### `merch/`
Artwork prepared specifically for apparel, embroidery, screen printing and other merchandise applications.

### `site/`
Web-ready identity and visual assets used by the JAGAVILLE site.

### `archive/`
Superseded assets that remain useful as part of JAGAVILLE's visual history.

## Production rule

Image generation may be used for visual exploration and mockups, but approved JAGAVILLE identity assets should be reconstructed and stored as deliberate, repeatable production files, with SVG as the preferred master format for logos and marks.

The mask and JAGAVILLE wordmark must remain usable independently and together across music, events, community work, merch, editorial projects and professional applications.
