# JAGAVILLE

JAGAVILLE is a movement and creative world built around the idea of **peace in chaos**.

The name draws from Nigerian slang, **jaga jaga**, meaning disorder or chaos, combined with **ville**, representing home, place and belonging. JAGAVILLE is intended to be bigger than one format: it can live through music, events, merch, community work, cyphers, a label, editorial ideas, digital experiences and other creative projects.

This repository currently contains the earliest version of the JAGAVILLE event website and will now be used as the working home for the evolving JAGAVILLE identity and site.

## Origin of the site

The earliest site in this repository originally lived under **iluvsluts.com**. That old version is being preserved as part of the visual and cultural history of JAGAVILLE rather than treated as disposable legacy code.

Its distorted monochrome, bootleg-internet and underground visual language remains useful reference material for the future JAGAVILLE identity.

## Core identity

- **Movement:** JAGAVILLE
- **Meaning:** Peace in chaos / home within disorder
- **Primary symbol:** The mask
- **Primary wordmark:** JAGAVILLE
- **Formal worldbuilding expression:** Republic of Jagaville
- **Artist relationship:** Trigga the 24

The mask is tied directly to Trigga the 24's masked identity and use of a balaclava. It should therefore be treated as a meaningful core symbol rather than a decorative event icon.

## Brand scope

The identity must be flexible enough to support:

- Music releases, including JAGAVILLE and JAGAVILLE II
- Events and nightlife
- Merch and apparel
- Community and charitable activity
- Cyphers and performances
- Label or music-industry activity
- Editorial and cultural projects
- Digital products and websites
- Physical IDs, passes, tickets and printed ephemera
