# JAGAVILLE Wordmark Directions

This document defines the initial wordmark exploration space for JAGAVILLE. It is intentionally focused on typography and structure rather than polished mockups.

The goal is to select a direction that can later be built as a proper vector wordmark and paired with the mask symbol.

## Design principles

The JAGAVILLE wordmark should feel like a movement rather than a single event, artist release, label, charity initiative or merch line.

It must be able to live across:

- Music releases
- Events and nightlife
- Cyphers and performances
- Merch and apparel
- Community work and charitable activity
- A future label or creative organisation
- Republic of Jagaville documents, IDs, passes and permits
- Editorial and cultural projects
- Digital products and websites

The core tension should remain:

- Official x underground
- Raw x controlled
- Personal x mysterious
- Chaotic x calm
- Nigerian-rooted x globally usable

The master wordmark should begin in black and white. Colour, texture and campaign treatments can come later.

## Direction 01 — State Modern

### Character
Clean, uppercase, disciplined and institutional. It should feel credible on an ID card, border document, official notice, state seal or building sign.

### Visual cues
- Uppercase construction
- Moderate to wide tracking
- Strong horizontal rhythm
- Neutral sans or grotesk foundation
- Minimal customisation
- Slightly formal proportions

### Strengths
- Strongest fit for the Republic/document system
- Extremely versatile
- Professional enough for serious events and community activity
- Easy to extend into sub-brands

### Risks
- Could become too cold or generic
- May lose some of the music and underground character

### Best use
Formal lockups, documents, signage, professional partnerships, ID systems.

---

## Direction 02 — Editorial Movement

### Character
Bold, cultural and contemporary. More like a music publication, movement poster or fashion-led cultural identity than a government department.

### Visual cues
- Condensed or semi-condensed proportions
- Strong typographic weight
- Tighter spacing
- Distinctive letter shapes
- Editorial headline energy
- Strong horizontal silhouette

### Strengths
- Strong for merch, music, posters and social
- Feels alive and movement-led
- More memorable than a neutral institutional wordmark

### Risks
- Can become too trend-led
- May feel less believable on official-looking documents

### Best use
Music releases, events, posters, editorial, merch and cultural communications.

---

## Direction 03 — Hybrid Institutional

### Character
The leading direction. A structured, official-feeling wordmark with enough custom detail to feel owned by JAGAVILLE rather than typeset from an off-the-shelf font.

### Visual cues
- Uppercase
- Medium to bold weight
- Controlled spacing
- Mostly clean geometry
- One or two custom letter interventions
- Strong but not aggressive
- Good balance between width and compression

### Potential custom-letter focus
- J: distinctive entry stroke or terminal
- A: custom apex or crossbar treatment
- G: unique inner construction
- V: strong central rhythm within the word
- Final E: possible subtle terminal treatment

### Strengths
- Best bridge between state/document language and music/culture
- Scales across formal and informal applications
- Can become a recognisable master wordmark
- Leaves room for the mask to carry more visual complexity

### Risks
- Requires careful custom drawing to avoid looking merely "nice"
- Needs disciplined production rather than image-generation approximation

### Best use
Primary JAGAVILLE wordmark across all applications.

---

## Direction 04 — Raw Cultural

### Character
Bootleg, stamped, photocopied and irregular. This direction reflects the jaga jaga side of the name more directly.

### Visual cues
- Compressed type
- Rough edges or distressed print treatment
- Stamped or overprinted feeling
- Uneven baseline or slight misregistration
- Strong black-and-white contrast

### Strengths
- Very expressive
- Strong underground identity
- Excellent for posters, campaign graphics, merch and archive-style pieces

### Risks
- Too unstable for the master wordmark
- Can become unreadable at small sizes
- Risks looking like an effect rather than a lasting identity

### Best use
Secondary wordmark treatment, event campaigns, limited merch, posters and social graphics.

---

## Direction 05 — Utility / Transit

### Character
Wayfinding, transport-system and public-information energy. JAGAVILLE feels like a real place people can enter, travel through and belong to.

### Visual cues
- Functional sans typography
- Clear spacing and legibility
- Route-map / station-sign logic
- Strong alignment
- Could support numbers, districts, issue codes and locations naturally

### Strengths
- Reinforces JAGAVILLE as a place and movement
- Excellent for wayfinding, events and physical environments
- Pairs naturally with visas, permits and entry systems

### Risks
- Could become too utilitarian
- Needs custom details to avoid feeling like generic transport branding

### Best use
Event environments, maps, signs, passes, site navigation and secondary identity applications.

---

## Direction 06 — Heritage / Republic

### Character
Ceremonial, archival and institutional. More historic than State Modern and better suited to formal state-worldbuilding.

### Visual cues
- Slightly more traditional proportions
- Possible serif or engraved influence
- Formal spacing
- Seal and document compatibility
- Archival or diplomatic tone

### Strengths
- Gives Republic of Jagaville depth and history
- Strong for formal document systems and ceremonial use
- Useful as a contrast to the more contemporary movement identity

### Risks
- Too formal for the main JAGAVILLE wordmark
- Could feel costume-like if overused

### Best use
Republic seals, certificates, special documents, ceremonial graphics and worldbuilding.

# Recommended exploration set

The first visual exploration should focus on three directions only:

1. **Hybrid Institutional** — strongest candidate for the master wordmark
2. **Editorial Movement** — strongest cultural alternative
3. **State Modern** — strongest formal alternative

Raw Cultural, Utility / Transit and Heritage / Republic should remain secondary systems that can later support campaign and worldbuilding applications.

# Evaluation criteria

Every visual wordmark direction should be judged against the same questions:

1. Does it feel like a movement?
2. Can it work beside the mask without competing with it?
3. Does it feel credible on an ID card or official document?
4. Does it still feel right on a hoodie or mixtape cover?
5. Can it work for an event, a cypher, a charity initiative or a future label?
6. Is it distinctive in black and white?
7. Does it remain legible at small size?
8. Can it be rebuilt precisely as vector geometry?
9. Does it feel like JAGAVILLE rather than a generic nightlife brand?
10. Does it balance peace and chaos rather than leaning entirely into aggression?

# Production rule

Image generation may be used for broad visual exploration only. The selected wordmark must be reconstructed deliberately as a vector asset with fixed geometry, spacing and proportions.

The final production deliverables should eventually include:

- Primary JAGAVILLE wordmark SVG
- Black-on-white version
- White-on-black version
- Horizontal lockup with mask
- Stacked lockup with mask
- Small-size version if needed
- Republic of Jagaville formal lockup
- Clear-space and minimum-size rules

# Current recommendation

Proceed with visual studies for Hybrid Institutional, Editorial Movement and State Modern, keeping all studies black and white and free of merch, poster or event mockup styling until the base wordmark direction is selected.
