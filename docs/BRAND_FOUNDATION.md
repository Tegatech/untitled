# JAGAVILLE Brand Foundation

## Status

Working brand foundation. This document is the current source of truth for identity exploration before final design production.

## Brand role

JAGAVILLE should feel like a **movement**.

It is not limited to an event brand, artist logo, clothing label or music project. It should be able to move naturally across all of those spaces while retaining one recognisable identity.

Possible future expressions include:

- Music and mixtapes
- Events
- Cyphers
- Merch
- Community initiatives and charitable activity
- A record label or creative platform
- Editorial projects
- Digital experiences
- Professional and cultural events

## Meaning

JAGAVILLE is a loose expression of **peace in chaos**.

The name combines:

- **jaga jaga**: Nigerian slang associated with disorder, mess or chaos
- **ville**: home, place, territory and belonging

The tension between those ideas is central to the brand: finding or building a home within disorder.

JAGAVILLE is also a deeply personal creative outlet and has already existed through music, including the JAGAVILLE mixtape, with JAGAVILLE II intended to continue that world.

## Artist relationship

JAGAVILLE is closely connected to **Trigga the 24**.

Trigga the 24 performs with a balaclava. The mask symbol therefore carries a direct relationship to the artist identity and should not be treated as a generic theatrical-mask graphic.

The identity should be able to stand independently of the artist while still feeling authentically connected to that origin.

## Identity hierarchy

### Master movement

**JAGAVILLE**

The wordmark should be the stable verbal identifier for the movement.

### Primary symbol

**The mask**

The mask is the main logo symbol and should ultimately function independently of the wordmark.

It must be strong enough for:

- Merch and embroidery
- ID cards and documents
- Website/favicon use
- Posters and social assets
- Stamps and seals
- Large-format event graphics
- One-colour reproduction

### Formal worldbuilding expression

**Republic of Jagaville**

This is an expression of the wider Jagaville world rather than the master brand itself. It can be used for IDs, visas, permits, notices, passes, tickets and other state/document-inspired applications.

## Core creative idea

Jagaville sits between:

- **Official × underground**
- **Raw × controlled**
- **Masked × personal**
- **Chaos × peace**
- **Street × intentional**
- **Mystery × belonging**

The visual identity should preserve these tensions rather than resolving everything into a polished corporate system.

## Visual world

### State and document language

Existing Jagaville references strongly support a fictional-state identity system using cues such as:

- IDs and licences
- Immigration and visa documents
- Issue and expiry dates
- Serial numbers and classification fields
- Stamps and signatures
- Security patterns
- Official notices
- Entry permits and travel documents

The aim is to abstract the language of official documentation rather than reproduce any real government document literally.

The tone can be formal on the surface while the content is playful or culturally specific, for example:

- Purpose of visit: Enjoyment
- Status: Cleared for entry
- Length of stay: Till sunrise
- Reason for entry: Vibes

### Event and cultural references

Current reference points include OVMBR, Sixtion and Recess.

Useful traits from this wider scene include:

- Strong lifestyle and crowd photography
- Flash, grain and imperfect documentation
- Bootleg-flyer and archival-print energy
- Editorial typography
- Nostalgic ephemera
- Travel, ticket and document motifs
- A world that feels culturally alive rather than templated

Jagaville should not imitate those brands directly. Its distinct advantage is the existing fictional-republic, masked-identity and document universe.

## Wordmark direction

The current preferred direction is **hybrid**.

It should balance:

- institutional/state authority
- music and cultural personality
- enough neutrality to work across professional and community contexts
- enough character to feel proprietary rather than typeset

Initial exploration should consider:

1. State Modern
2. Editorial Movement
3. Hybrid Institutional
4. Raw Cultural as a secondary treatment rather than the master wordmark

The master wordmark should remain simpler than the mask so the two assets complement one another.

## Mask direction

The final mask should preserve the spirit and structure of the original Jagaville mask rather than becoming a generic polished emblem.

Requirements:

- One-colour black-and-white master first
- Recognisable at small scale
- Strong silhouette
- Suitable for embroidery and printing
- Works black on white and white on black
- Closer to the original interlocking-mask structure
- Somewhere between abstract emblem and balaclava-inspired identity
- Not overly aggressive, because peace within chaos is central to the brand

The Piano People logo is a useful reference for **design-system logic**: compact symbol, bold silhouette, strong one-colour use and portability. It is not a reference to copy visually.

## Production rule

Image generation may be used for **direction studies and mockups only**.

Final Jagaville identity assets should be deliberately constructed as production-ready vectors so the chosen design does not change between concept and execution.

Expected master outputs eventually include:

- SVG wordmark
- SVG mask symbol
- Combined lockups
- Black and white variants
- Reverse variants
- Republic/document lockup
- Favicon/icon exports
- Merch-ready assets

## Current design sequence

1. Maintain this brand foundation as the source of truth
2. Explore and choose the JAGAVILLE wordmark direction
3. Construct the final wordmark as vector artwork
4. Reconstruct/refine the mask as a production vector
5. Define combined lockups and spacing rules
6. Build the Republic/document expression
7. Test on IDs, merch, website and event applications

## Principle

The movement remains stable. The expression can change.

A JAGAVILLE cypher, charity initiative, mixtape, event, record label or hoodie should all be able to look different while still unmistakably belonging to the same world.
